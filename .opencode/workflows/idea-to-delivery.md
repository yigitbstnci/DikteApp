# Workflow: Idea to Delivery

## Purpose

Move a software product request from unclear idea to validated delivery.

## Inputs

- Product idea or feature request
- Target users
- Constraints and success criteria

## Steps

1. Clarify problem, users, outcome, and constraints.
2. Define MVP scope and non-goals.
3. Identify missing context or specialist agent needs.
4. Draft product behavior and technical approach.
5. Break work into small implementation tasks.
6. Validate with checklist, tests, and review.
7. Deliver summary, limitations, and next steps.

## Validation

- Scope is clear.
- Delivery checklist is complete or gaps are documented.
- Agent recommendations are justified, not automatic.
